The BRIDGE survey

Baryonic Enrichment in Diffuse Gaseous Environments

BRIDGE traces the baryon cycle between galaxies and the gas that surrounds them, connecting the circumgalactic medium (CGM) and the intergalactic medium (IGM) at cosmic noon ($z \sim 2$–$3$), the epoch when star formation and black hole growth in the Universe peaked.

Motivation

Galaxies are not closed systems. Gas flows in along filaments, is processed into stars, and is returned—enriched with metals and energy—to the surrounding medium through winds and outflows. The diffuse gas that mediates this exchange, spanning the CGM out to the IGM, holds the majority of a galaxy's baryonic and metal budget, yet it remains the least directly constrained component of galaxy evolution.

BRIDGE uses absorption-line spectroscopy of background quasars and galaxies to map this gas in column density, kinematics, and metallicity as a function of galaxy environment, stellar mass, and distance from the host halo.

The metal budget

A central goal of the survey is to close the baryonic and metal budget around star-forming galaxies at cosmic noon. We define the metal mass retained in the CGM relative to the metal mass produced by star formation as

$$ f_{\mathrm{Z,CGM}} \;=\; \frac{M_{Z,\mathrm{CGM}}}{y \, M_{\star}} $$

where $M_{Z,\mathrm{CGM}}$ is the metal mass inferred from absorption-line column densities integrated over the halo, $y$ is the nucleosynthetic yield, and $M_{\star}$ is the galaxy's stellar mass. Comparing $f_{\mathrm{Z,CGM}}$ across the sample lets us test whether metals lost from galaxies are retained locally in the CGM, vented further into the IGM, or some combination of both, as a function of halo mass and star formation history.

CGM and IGM absorption

For each sightline we measure the total neutral hydrogen column density from the curve of growth,

$$ N(\mathrm{H\,\textsc{i}}) \;=\; \frac{m_e c}{\pi e^2 f \lambda_0} \int \tau_v \, dv, $$

together with associated metal-line species (e.g. $\mathrm{C\,\textsc{iv}}$, $\mathrm{Si\,\textsc{iv}}$, $\mathrm{O\,\textsc{vi}}$) to constrain ionization state and metallicity via photoionization modeling. Sightlines are selected to probe a range of impact parameters, from the inner CGM through to the diffuse IGM, allowing a continuous picture of the enrichment gradient around galaxies at this epoch.
